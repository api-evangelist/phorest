An aesthetic clinic needs more than basic booking software once operational complexity outpaces the calendar, typically, when it adds a second or third practitioner, opens a second location, widens its treatment menu, or starts spending real money on marketing it needs to measure. Revenue growth alone isn’t the trigger. Complexity is.
