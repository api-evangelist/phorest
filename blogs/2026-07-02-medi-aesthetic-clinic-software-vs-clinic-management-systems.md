Quick answer: Clinic management systems are built around clinical documentation, billing, and patient records. Aesthetic clinic management software is built around the full client journey, while still supporting the clinical workflows a medi aesthetics business needs. Independent aesthetic clinics focused on growth typically need the latter, or a platform that combines both.
